# VELORA · Above the Clouds

Project 01 of 10. VELORA is a fictional mountain lodge in the Georgian Caucasus.

## Film brief

Use `approach.webp` as the first frame and `lounge.webp` as the last frame if the generator supports both. Export an MP4 in landscape 16:9, ideally 1080p.

> A single continuous cinematic camera movement at human eye level, about 8–12 seconds. Start on the stone path looking at the black timber lodge at blue hour. Glide forward along the path, enter through the amber-lit door at the midpoint, then continue into the dark timber lounge and settle facing the panoramic windows and snowy Caucasus. Preserve the architecture, landscape and time of day from the references. Natural motion and consistent perspective. No cuts, dissolves, text, logos, people, or sudden changes in speed.

Use **Test an MP4** on the page to scrub through a local export. The selected video stays on the device. To ship it, place `velora.mp4` here and set `<body data-film="velora.mp4">` in `index.html`. The page presents two stills until a film is available. For web delivery use H.264, fast-start metadata, and frequent keyframes to make seeking responsive.
