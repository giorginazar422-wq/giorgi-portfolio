# VELORA · Above the Clouds

Project 01 of 10. VELORA is a fictional mountain lodge in the Georgian Caucasus.

The 11-second film animates three generated stills with camera push-ins and soft transitions. The page maps scrolling to the film in both directions. It shows the stills while the film loads and when reduced motion is enabled.

Use **Test an MP4** to preview another export locally. To replace the shipped film, overwrite `velora.mp4` or point `data-film` in `index.html` at the new file. For responsive seeking, use H.264 with fast-start metadata and frequent keyframes.
