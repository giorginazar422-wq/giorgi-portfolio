# Scroll World · 10 concepts by Giorgi Khuchua

All brands below are fictional portfolio concepts.

| # | Brand | Industry | Scroll journey |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | VELORA | Mountain lodge | A foggy stone trail leads through an amber-lit doorway into a lounge overlooking the Caucasus. |
| 02 | Ember & Dough | Pizzeria | Flour hits the counter, the camera follows a pizza into the wood-fired oven, and the finished slice lands on a table. |
| 03 | Kartli Clay | Ceramics studio | A lump of clay spins into a bowl, passes through a kiln and appears in a sunlit showroom. |
| 04 | Black Pine Audio | Headphones | The camera enters the earcup, passes the driver and emerges in a listening studio. |
| 05 | Tideglass | Ocean observatory | A glass elevator descends from the surface to a room overlooking a reef. |
| 06 | Rail & Ridge | Scenic railway | Depart a valley station, cross a stone viaduct and arrive at a snowy mountain pass. |
| 07 | Nocturne Atelier | Perfume house | A night garden leads to a distillation room and then to a glass bottle on a black stone plinth. |
| 08 | Kera Frame | Architectural studio | Follow a pencil line on a drawing into a scale model that becomes a full-size courtyard. |
| 09 | Vanta Volt | Electric bicycle | Approach the bike on a workbench, pass through the motor assembly and follow the ride onto a forest track. |
| 10 | Echo Vault | Music museum | Walk through a record sleeve into a recording booth, then emerge onstage in an empty concert hall. |
